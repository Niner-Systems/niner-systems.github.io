Form factor is always an interesting choice. Looking at the existing products on the market, we noticed everything was lots of wires. Connecting a headset that already has wires into a device through yet another wire just adds more.

We thought about how we'd want our own home simulators set up. Mounting the adapter to the underside of a desk or the side of a Honeycomb yoke was much more appealing than having it dangle in the air on zip ties, lie on the desk, or fall behind it.

We tested a few different enclosure sizes. We could have gone smaller or larger, but we felt this size was a great middle ground: small enough to take with your laptop, and large enough that when it's mounted, it's a sturdy place to plug in and unplug your headset without holding the adapter. (Exact dimensions are in the [USB2GA specifications](/usb2ga/#specs).)
