In the airplane, the intercom plays your own voice back into your headset &mdash; that's **sidetone**, and it's what makes radio calls feel natural. Computers don't do this by default.

The free **[USB2x Toolbar](/downloads/toolbar/)** app for the USB2GA and USB2Lemo adds it:

- **Sidetone on/off** in a single click
- **Smart VOX (voice activated):** sidetone follows your voice, with adjustable squelch so the headset stays silent until you speak
- **Sidetone volume** control (Windows)
- **Separate microphone and speaker levels**, right from your computer's toolbar

It runs natively on **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel) and **Windows 10 / 11**, detects your adapter automatically, and can load at startup. On Windows it's available from the Microsoft Store or as a direct .msi download.

**Note:** adapters sold before May 1, 2026 may not be compatible with sidetone support.
