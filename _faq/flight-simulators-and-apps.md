The adapter behaves like a USB sound card. Once it's plugged in, it appears in your system's sound settings, and any program that lets you choose an audio input and output can use it:

- **Simulators:** Microsoft Flight Simulator (MSFS), X-Plane
- **Online ATC networks:** PilotEdge, VATSIM, IVAO
- **Tablet apps:** ForeFlight and other EFB apps on an iPad with USB-C

Set the adapter as both the **output (speaker)** and **input (microphone)** device &mdash; either system-wide or inside the simulator's audio settings.

**Want to hear your own voice?** Real aircraft intercoms play your voice back into your headset (sidetone). The free [USB2x Toolbar](/downloads/toolbar/) app adds that &mdash; see [How do I hear my own voice in the headset?](/faq/sidetone/)
