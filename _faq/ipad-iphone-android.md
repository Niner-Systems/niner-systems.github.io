In addition to **Windows (7 / 10 / 11), macOS, and Linux** (we've tested Ubuntu and Raspberry Pi), the USB2GA and USB2Lemo work with **iPhones, iPads, and Android devices with USB-C ports**. Plug the included USB-C cable into the device and it shows up as an audio device &mdash; no app or driver required.

A few ways pilots use it:

- **Preflight your headset setup at home.** Verify your Bluetooth connections to your iPad and phone, test that ForeFlight alerts come through, and practice on VATSIM, IVAO, or PilotEdge with your own headset.
- **Travel.** Flying commercial but taking your headset along? Use it for movies or games on the go. (We know you're secretly hoping they'll ask over the intercom, *"Is there another pilot on board?"*)
