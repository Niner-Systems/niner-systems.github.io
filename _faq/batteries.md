No batteries and no wall adapter. The adapter draws power from your computer, tablet, or phone's USB port and uses it to run the audio electronics and to generate the **+12V phantom power (mic bias)** that aviation headset microphones need.

**About ANR and Bluetooth:** the adapter doesn't power your headset's active noise reduction or Bluetooth. Headsets with GA dual plugs normally run ANR from their own batteries anyway. Headsets with a 6-pin Lemo plug normally get **panel power** from the aircraft &mdash; the [USB2Lemo doesn't provide panel power](/faq/lemo-panel-power-headsets/), so those features need the headset's batteries when you use it at your desk.
