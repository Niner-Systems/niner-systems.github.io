## What USB2Lemo provides

[USB2Lemo](/usb2lemo/) is the same mountable adapter as the USB2GA, with a round 6-pin Lemo jack on the front instead of GA dual-plug jacks. It carries your headset's audio and microphone over USB-C and supplies the **+12V phantom power (mic bias)** aviation headset microphones require. It's powered entirely by the USB port &mdash; no batteries or wall adapter for the adapter itself.

## What it does not provide: panel power

In an aircraft, the 6-pin Lemo plug also delivers **panel power** from the aircraft to the headset. Panel power is what runs a headset's **ANR (active noise reduction)** and **Bluetooth** features when you're in the plane.

**USB2Lemo does not supply panel power.** If your headset has ANR or Bluetooth, those features will still need the headset's own batteries when you use it with USB2Lemo. Audio and the microphone work either way.
